Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 10, 2026

You can request permanent deletion of your Sensi Hub account and its associated data at any time. Sensi Hub does not yet support deleting your account directly inside the app, so deletion requests are handled by email as described below.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to alynostore@gmail.com from the same Google account you use to sign in to Sensi Hub.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete My Sensi Hub Account".
  3. Include your in-app username so we can locate your account.

We'll confirm and complete your request within 7 business days.

What Gets Deleted

DataOutcome
Account profile (name, email, username, avatar)Permanently deleted
Sensitivities you publishedPermanently deleted
Your likes, saves, and notificationsPermanently deleted
Premium subscription statusDeleted (cancel billing separately via Google Play if subscribed)

Locally-stored data on your device (such as your interactions with the App's built-in starter sensitivities) is removed automatically when you uninstall the App.

Retention

Once a deletion request is confirmed, your data is removed from our production systems within 7 business days and from backups within 30 days.
