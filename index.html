Sensi Hub

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Sensi Hub ("the App", "we", "us") is a community app that lets Free Fire players browse, create, and share controller/touch sensitivity settings for different devices. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account profile — name, email address, and profile picture — to create and identify your Sensi Hub account. We also store a username you choose and your activity within the App (sensitivities you publish, likes, saves, and follower-style notifications).

Content you create

Sensitivity settings, titles, and descriptions you publish are stored on our servers and are visible to other users of the App, along with your username.

Device and usage data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as screens viewed and features used) to understand how the App is used and to improve it. Some preferences and locally-generated content (such as the App's built-in starter sensitivities and their like counts) are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Advertising identifiers

The App shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy below for details on how this data is handled.

Purchase information

If you subscribe to Sensi Hub Premium, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We only receive confirmation that a subscription is active — we never see or store your payment card or billing details.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and display your published content to other users.
  • To operate core features: likes, saves, notifications, and search.
  • To show and measure ads for free-tier users.
  • To grant and maintain Premium subscription benefits.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the App's features and performance.
  • To detect abuse, spam, and content that violates our community standards.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above, as necessary to operate the App, or when required by law.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and content for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time — see our Account Deletion page for instructions, or contact us using the email below. Some locally-stored data (such as your interactions with the App's offline starter content) lives only on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your Choices

  • You can edit your profile username at any time from within the App.
  • You can delete sensitivities you've published at any time.
  • You can request full account deletion by contacting us.
  • You can limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
